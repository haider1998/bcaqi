About BCAQI

🚀

India's Premier Deep Tech Community

BCAQI (Bharat Centre for AI & Quantum InfoHub) is a globally distributed, institution-grade ecosystem connecting top researchers, innovators, and leaders in AI and Quantum Computing.

Founded in Bangalore, we've grown into a vibrant network spanning 15+ countries, bringing together 20,000+ individuals passionate about shaping the future of technology.

🎯
