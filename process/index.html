Process, communication & security

How we work removes the anxiety of offshore delivery.

Most offshore risk is communication risk. We manage it with a reporting discipline familiar to Japanese teams, a clear security posture, and unambiguous IP terms.

Communication

The single biggest source of offshore risk — managed deliberately.

Our reporting follows the 報連相 (hō-ren-sō) discipline familiar to Japanese teams: proactive reporting (報告), immediate contact (連絡) the moment an issue appears, and consultation (相談) before decisions are made.

  • →Weekly written progress report — scope, status, blockers, next steps. In English and Japanese.
  • →Daily async check-in — a short written update so you are never left guessing.
  • →Zero-surprise policy — you hear about a problem from us before it becomes one.
  • →Response within 24 hours on JST business days.

Quality

Definition of done, agreed up front.

  • →Definition of done written before work begins, so "finished" is not a matter of opinion.
  • →Code review standard — every change is reviewed before it reaches your environment.
  • →Testing approach — automated tests on the logic that matters; evaluation sets for AI accuracy.
  • →Staged demos at weeks 1, 2, 3, and 4 of a PoC — you see progress, not just a final reveal.

Security

Your data stays where you agree it can.

  • →NDA before any data exchange — standard on every engagement, no exceptions.
  • →Access-controlled repositories — least-privilege access, named individuals only.
  • →Agreed environments only — client data is processed only where you approve, including inside your own infrastructure.
  • →Deletion on project close — data and derived artifacts removed on completion, confirmed in writing.

Intellectual property

Stated plainly — a top concern for Japanese buyers.

All work-product IP transfers to the client on payment. The code, the models, and the documentation are yours.

Timezone

Geography as an advantage, not a compromise.

India (IST) is only −3.5 hours from Japan (JST). Our morning overlaps your afternoon, so feedback you send during your working day gets a same-day turnaround — not a 12-hour wait like US or European offshore teams.
