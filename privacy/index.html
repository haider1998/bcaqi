Privacy & data handling

Plain-language summary of what we collect and how we use it. Last updated 2026.

What we collect

When you use the contact form, we receive the details you enter — your name, company, email, inquiry type, and message. We use them only to respond to your inquiry. We do not add you to a marketing list and we do not sell or share your details.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled on this site, we use a privacy-respecting analytics tool that measures aggregate page visits without tracking you across other websites and without selling your data.

The document intelligence demonstration

The demo on the Work page runs entirely in your browser. The preloaded sample documents contain no real personal data. Any file you choose to upload is used only to display the demo in your browser and is not sent to a server by the demonstration.

Client project data

For paid engagements, data handling is governed by the NDA and agreement we sign with you: an NDA is signed before any data is exchanged, data is processed only in environments you approve, and data and derived artifacts are deleted on project close, confirmed in writing.

Contact

Questions about privacy or a request relating to your data: contact@bcaqi.com.
