Questions & answers

White-label AI delivery — FAQ

What is a white-label AI delivery partner?

A white-label AI delivery partner builds AI systems that a consulting firm or system integrator delivers to their own client under their own brand. BCAQI Labs provides the engineering capacity — a dedicated pod of 2–3 engineers plus a lead architect — while the partner keeps ownership of the client relationship. We never contact the end client directly.

Does my client ever know you are involved?

Only if you choose to disclose it. We deliver under your brand, report to your team, and follow your naming and process. The client relationship stays yours.

How does the dedicated AI pod scale?

The pod is billed monthly and scales up or down as your pipeline changes. It gives consulting firms and SIs AI delivery capacity without the hiring risk of building an in-house AI team.

How do you report and protect confidentiality?

Weekly written reporting following 報連相, an NDA before any data exchange, access-controlled repositories, and IP transfer to you (and onward to your client) on payment.
